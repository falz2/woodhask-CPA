What we do

01 / ASSURANCE

Audit & assurance

Tailored, partner-led audits and independent assurance to strengthen confidence in financial statements and key controls. We support audits designed around each organisation and maintain quality through layered review and professional development. IFRS support includes training, technical guidance and regular updates.

  • Financial statement audits
  • Internal control reports
  • Agreed-upon procedures
  • Project and donor audits
  • Special attestation reports
  • IFRS and ISA training/support
  • IT general and automated controls

02 / TAX

Tax advisory

Tax specialists support compliance and help you understand exposure before high-risk transactions are completed. We provide practical local and international planning and keep clients informed throughout assignments.

  • Transfer-pricing policies and documentation
  • Tax health checks
  • Withholding-tax exemptions
  • Tax computation and returns
  • Tax planning and restructuring
  • Tax registration and deregistration
  • Tax rulings, appeals and objections

03 / FINANCE OPERATIONS

Accounting & outsourcing

Confidential, coordinated outsourcing that frees your organisation to focus on its strategic priorities. Support ranges from reliable day-to-day records to financial reporting and remote CFO capacity.

  • Bookkeeping and financial accounting
  • Payroll and personnel administration
  • IFRS financial statements
  • Remote CFO services
  • Monthly compliance

04 / ADVISORY

Advisory & risk

We help organisations solve problems, manage risk, respond to regulation and identify ways to improve operations or protect value.

  • Internal audit
  • Compliance and regulatory checks
  • Forensic investigations
  • Accounting irregularities and misconduct
  • Corporate recovery and restructuring
  • Debt recovery and collections
  • Human-resource audits and planning
